## Templates

### Research plan (one page)

```text
Background:        why now, what we know
Research questions: 3 to 5, each answerable
Method:            and why it fits the questions
Participants:      criteria, number, recruiting, incentive
Schedule:          sessions, synthesis, readout
Materials:         consent form, script or tasks, recording setup
Outputs:           insights report, updated journey map, recommendations
```

### Insight statement

```text
We observed <pattern> in <n of N> participants (<segment>).
This matters because <impact on goal or user>.
We recommend <design response>, measured by <metric>.
Evidence: <session IDs, clips, data links>
```
