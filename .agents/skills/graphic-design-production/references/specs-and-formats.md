## Print basics (confirm with the printer)

- Resolution: 300 ppi at final size for images (higher for line art); check effective resolution after scaling.
- Bleed: commonly 3 mm (Europe) or 0.125 in (US); keep text and key elements inside a safe margin (commonly 3 to 5 mm from trim).
- Rich black for large areas per printer spec; 100 percent K only for small black text to avoid misregistration.
- Total ink coverage limit per the printer's profile (often 300 to 340 percent).
- Paper sizes: ISO 216 (A4 210 by 297 mm) internationally, US Letter (8.5 by 11 in) in North America. [ISO 216](https://www.iso.org/standard/36631.html).
