# Graphic Design Production

Follow [13-visual-assets-and-licensing](../../rules/13-visual-assets-and-licensing.md) and [09-design-principles](../../rules/09-design-principles.md). Specifications, formats and references are in [references/specs-and-formats.md](references/specs-and-formats.md). Photo retouching and web image export are in [photo-editing-and-retouching](../photo-editing-and-retouching/SKILL.md); video and animated pieces are in [video-editing-and-post-production](../video-editing-and-post-production/SKILL.md) and [motion-graphics-and-animation](../motion-graphics-and-animation/SKILL.md).

## 1. Brief

Capture: purpose and the single message, audience, where it appears (channel, placement, size, viewing distance, print or screen), brand guidelines, mandatory elements (logo, legal text, call to action, QR code), deadline, file deliverables, and approval steps. Look up current size and safe-zone specifications in the platform's or printer's official documentation and note the date.

## 2. Concept

1. Research the context: the brand, competitors, the category's visual conventions, and what the audience already recognises.
2. Develop two or three distinct concepts as thumbnails before refining any. Present each with its reasoning.
3. Choose with the stakeholder against the brief, not taste alone.

## 3. Design

- **Hierarchy**: one focal point; the message readable in the first seconds; supporting information grouped; the call to action obvious.
- **Typography**: a limited set of families and weights from the brand; sizes legible at the real viewing size (check on a phone at 100 percent for social; at distance for posters); comfortable leading and tracking; no fake bold or italic; licensed fonts only.
- **Colour**: brand palette; sufficient contrast for text over images (overlay, scrim or solid panel); colour-blind-safe distinctions; specified in the right colour space for the medium.
- **Layout**: a grid and margins; consistent spacing; alignment; safe zones for platform UI overlays and print trim.
- **Imagery**: licensed, high-resolution, consistent treatment; people represented respectfully and diversely; no misleading edits of real events or people.
- **Logos and identity**: simple, distinctive and legible at small sizes (favicon, app icon) and large; versions for horizontal, stacked, icon-only, single colour, reversed; clear space and minimum size rules; tested in black and white first. Run a trademark search before adoption (the client's lawyer decides).

## 4. Produce and export

1. Build in a layered, editable source file with styles, components or symbols, and linked (not pasted) high-resolution images.
2. Export per specification ([references/specs-and-formats.md](references/specs-and-formats.md)): format, dimensions, colour profile, resolution, compression, file naming.
3. Print: confirm the printer's spec sheet, convert to the specified CMYK profile or keep spot colours, add bleed and crop marks as requested, embed fonts, export PDF/X, and preflight (Acrobat Preflight or the design app's preflight). Ask for a proof for colour-critical jobs.
4. Digital: sRGB, optimised files within the page's weight budget, alt text written for each image placed on the web or in email, live text instead of text in images where the channel allows.

## 5. Accessibility of graphics

- Text in graphics meets contrast (4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text) and is large enough at the real display size.
- Information is not conveyed by colour alone (charts, maps, status).
- Every image used digitally has alt text; complex graphics (infographics) have a text version.
- Video and animation: captions, no flashing, a static alternative.

## 6. Deliver

- [ ] Brief and specification met; sizes and safe zones checked against the current official spec
- [ ] Fonts, images and icons licensed for this use; sources and licences recorded
- [ ] Correct colour space and profile per medium; print files preflighted
- [ ] Files named consistently and packaged (source, exports, fonts licence notes, brand guide pages if created)
- [ ] Alt text and accessibility checks done for digital pieces
